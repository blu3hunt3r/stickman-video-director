# THIN ICE

A 3:06 vertical (1080×1920, 24 fps) wordless thriller in Saul Bass cut paper. Scored, no voices
but breath.

**Logline.** On a winter night a mother carries her small daughter across a frozen border river,
followed by a guard who reads their tracks in the snow. She lays a false trail that ends at the
river's thin black ice. He follows it and falls through, and she has to choose whether to save
the man who was hunting them. She saves him with her daughter's red scarf. He sees the child,
opens his watch on the photograph of his own daughter, and lets them go. He tells the other
guards they drowned. Fresh snow covers every track.

**The theme, in the title.** THIN ICE is the river, the trick and the moral ground every one of
them walks on. The title cracks; a thin red thread holds the two halves together. That thread is
the scarf.

## The story in one breath

Snow falls on a border at night: a fence, a watchtower whose searchlight sweeps the snow, a
guardhouse, woods, a frozen river, and on the far bank one farmhouse window, lit. Two small
figures crouch at the fence. **The snow stops.** The clouds part, the moon comes out, and the
whole white page turns bright: from now on every step will show.

A mother and her daughter wait for the searchlight to pass, and the girl crawls under the wire.
The music box in her pocket springs open and tinkles three notes of a lullaby; the mother slaps
it shut, finger to lips. The light swings back and slides over them, flat in the snow, and on.
The mother crawls under; her coat snags on a barb and a scrap of it tears away and stays there.
They run for the woods. From above, two lines of footprints, big and small, lead from the fence
into the trees.

In the guardhouse the guard warms his hands at the stove. He opens his pocket watch: in its lid,
a small photograph of a girl in a pompom hat. He closes it, takes his lantern and rifle and goes
out on his round. At the fence his lantern finds the scrap on the wire, then the prints, big and
small. He blows his whistle; far off, lanterns wake. He follows the prints into the woods.

The girl falls in the deep snow. Her mother lifts her onto her back and the small prints end; the
big ones go on, deeper. The guard's lantern finds the place and understands. The mother breaks
out of the trees at the river. Across the ice is the farmhouse light. In the middle of the white
river runs a band of black ice. She throws a stone onto it and it punches straight through:
black water. **The dark ice is thin.** Upstream, where the ice is white all the way across, the
searchlight sweeps. Behind her, the lantern is coming.

So she writes a lie on the page. Carrying the girl, she walks straight out across the white ice
to the edge of the black band, stops, and walks **backward in her own footprints**, boot into
print, to the fallen tree that lies beside her trail; steps up onto the trunk, which takes no
prints, walks along it to the bank and hides in the reeds. One line of prints now leads from
the woods out onto the river and ends at the black ice, as if they had crossed.

The guard follows the trail with his lantern, reading it, past the trunk, past the reeds where
two pairs of eyes watch him. The prints end. He lifts his lantern: beyond the black ice, the
farmhouse light. They crossed. He steps onto the black ice. In the reeds the mother half rises
to stop him, too late. **The ice breaks.** Under it, in the black water, his lantern sinks away
glowing, his rifle after it; he sees the hole of light above and fights up to it, and clings to
an edge that keeps breaking.

The way upstream is open. The mother takes her daughter's hand to go. The girl doesn't move. She
is looking at the hole. She unwinds her red scarf and holds it up to her mother.

The mother crawls out along the trunk, flat, and throws the scarf: a red line across the black
ice. It falls short. She throws again; he catches it. She hooks her boot behind a branch and
pulls, hand over hand, and he slides out of the water along the red line, flat on the ice, to
the trunk, to the bank, and lies there gasping.

He fumbles in his coat and she shields the girl, but it is the watch he takes out. Water runs
out of it; it has stopped. In its lid, the photograph of his daughter. He looks up from her to
the girl. Whistles: lanterns in the trees, close. He points upstream: go. He wraps the scarf
back round the girl's neck, and they go. When the other guards come down to the river he is
standing over the hole that the trail leads into. He points at it and shakes his head. They
lower their lanterns, and one takes off his hat. **It begins to snow.**

Upstream, in the falling snow, the mother carries her daughter across the white ice. On the far
bank a farmhouse door opens on warm light and a woman waves them in. In the window the girl
opens the music box and the lullaby plays all the way through. Over the river the snow fills the
footprints, the trail, the hole, until the page is blank. Only the red thread is left.

**Value plan.** Figures read best as ink on snow, so most profile shots set the horizon high and
stand the figures against the white page; the `NIGHT` sky is a band at the top. Where a figure is
against the sky, it gets its `SNOW` rim. Light reads the page: moonlit snow is `SNOW`; where the
searchlight falls it turns `PAPER`; where the lantern falls it warms (an `AMBER` pool added on
top); prints are `SHADOW` and read crisper inside a light.

**Colour script.** s01–s07 cold: `NIGHT`, `SNOW`, `ICE`, the searchlight's white, and the only
warmth is the hunter's lantern. s08 the warmth sinks away. s09–s10 the red scarf. s11–s12 the
farmhouse door's `AMBER` takes them in; the page goes white; the red thread is the last thing.

### Paper

OPPOSITE's settings: `bass.bass_scene(f, field=..., paper=0.85, vignette=0.12, mottle=0.035,
flicker=0.0)`, grain on twos (`f.post["grain_fps"] = 12.0`). Crisp cut edges, no blur, no
gradients, no glow except the flat nested pools of light.

### Camera: the snow is a page, and light reads it

One idea for the whole film. Every scene moves between two kinds of shot:

- **The story:** flat profile tableaux at eye level (elevations; at the river, sections through
  the ice). The fugitives always travel **screen right** (north). Close-ups of eyes, hands and
  boots.
- **The page:** straight down on the snow. North is **up**, so the journey climbs the 9:16 frame:
  fence at the bottom, woods, the river, the farmhouse at the top. Footprints are the writing;
  the moon, the searchlight and the lantern are what reads it. Figures from above are small ink
  shapes trailing long moon shadows.

Cuts between them are hard cuts on the beat, often matched on a point (a boot pressing into
snow, then its print from above). Moves are eased (`inout3`, `out3`); push-ins and pull-backs
are slow. **One Dutch tilt**, in panic, when the ice gives way (s08). **Shake only on impact:**
the crack and the break (s08). **One rising crane**, at the very end, as the page goes blank
(s12).

### Motifs

- **Lines and circles.** The lines divide: the fence, the wire, the border river, the black band,
  the crack, the trail. The circles look: the moon, the searchlight's disc, the lantern's pool,
  the hole, the watch. The red scarf is the one line that joins.
- **Writing on the page.** Footprints are the film's handwriting. The false trail is a lie
  written on it; the snow wipes the page clean at the end.

## Sound plan (for the mix)

- **Score:** strings and a music box, 120 bpm, **A minor**, ending in **A major**.
  - **The lullaby** (the theme; music box): a simple falling-and-rising tune, one note per two
    beats. Suggested: E5 C5 | D5 B4 | C5 A4 | B4 E4 | A4 C5 | B4 G#4 | A4 (held). Its first three
    notes (E5 C5 D5) are what the music box plays in s02 before the mother snaps it shut. The
    strings carry fragments of it, low and slow, through the hunt. The whole tune is heard once,
    in s12, on the music box, in A major (E5 C#5 D5 B4 ...), with the strings under it.
  - The hunt (s03–s07): low sustained strings, a soft low pulse on the beat that quickens with
    the chase, high harmonics for the ice; the heartbeat in s07.
  - s08: silence for a beat after the crack; underwater, the score drops to a low drone and the
    heartbeat; it surfaces with him.
  - s09: the heartbeat alone, with one held high note; it stops when she takes the scarf; one note
    of the lullaby (the first, E5) on the music box.
  - s10: the strings build under the pull; a release when he is out.
  - s11: quiet; the watch makes no sound; the whistles; a low chord under the lie; the first
    flakes with a shimmer.
  - s12: the lullaby; the strings resolve to A major; silence from 13.75.
- **The ice** (the film's signature, synthesised): deep groans and booms; dispersive "pew"
  chirps (a sweep from high to low in 0.1–0.4 s, the sound of lake ice); sharp cracks; the break
  and the plunge; small crunches as the edge breaks; the stone's crack and plop.
- **Foley:** wind (always, louder in the open, dropping when the snow stops), three distinct snow
  crunches (the mother soft and quick; the girl small and quicker; the guard heavy and slow, with
  hobnails), the searchlight's motor hum and its clank at each reversal, wire creak, the music box
  lid's snap, cloth rip, the stove's crackle, the watch's click and tick, the door, the whistle,
  the lantern's creak and swing, the trunk's creak, splashes, bubbles, the river under the ice,
  the scarf's whip as it is thrown.
- **Breath:** the recordings in `films/opposite/audio/gen` (`her_breath`, `his_breath`, `gasp_a`,
  `gasp_b`, `stifled`, `body_fall`) and `films/double_cross/audio/gen/grunt_knight.mp3` for his
  effort. **No new paid generation.**
- **Mix:** −16 LUFS integrated, true peak ≤ −1.3 dBTP, short-term loudness kept above about −29
  LUFS everywhere except the designed silences (s08's beat after the crack; the end), digital
  silence from s12 13.75 to the end.

## Technical

- **Package:** `films/thin_ice/` (`--film thin_ice`).
- **Scene files:** `scenes/sNN_slug.py` with:
  - `render(f, t, T)`, which calls `K.scene_setup(f, field)` first and then draws inside
    `with f.screen() as c:`;
  - `cues(T)`, a list of `engine.timing.Cue`;
  - `motion_blur(t, T)`, which returns 1;
  - beat constants at module level (`T_CRACK = 0.0`, and so on).
- **Imports:**
  - `from films.thin_ice import kit as K` (palette, paper, devices, snow, lights, prints, cracks);
  - `from films.thin_ice import world as W` (the map, the plan camera, the elevations, the
    record of footprints);
  - `from films.thin_ice import cast as C` (the figures, in profile and from above; props);
  - `from films.thin_ice import faces as F` (the close-ups).
- **Stills:** `RENDER_WORKERS=1 python3 -m engine.render --film thin_ice sNN --times=1.0,2.5 --scale 1.0`
  (outputs in `films/thin_ice/qa/sNN/`).
- **Contact sheet:** `RENDER_WORKERS=1 python3 -m engine.render --film thin_ice sNN --sheet 12 --window 2:6 --scale 0.25`.
- **Budget:** at most 1.5 s per full-size frame. Deterministic: no unseeded random.
- **Text:** no text in picture except the title.
- **Self-review:** look at frames at full size. Check hands, edges, seams, popping, foot sliding,
  readability at phone size, and that every beat reads in one glance without the sound.
