# SALVAGE: production bible

> **Logline.** A deep-sea diver is paid in cash to bring up a sunken captain's safe for a
> man who never asks his name. The man is the shipowner who sank her. The captain was the
> diver's father. Run time 3:01.5 (4356 frames at 24 fps), 9:16.

Read this together with `STYLE.md` (the binding style contract), `engine/API.md`, and
`films/salvage/timeline/timeline.json` (locked timing, generated by
`timeline/build_timeline.py`). Narration is sparse. Long wordless stretches belong to the
picture, so make them breathe.

## 1. What the film is about

The sea keeps the truth until someone comes back for it. Every image is a **descent** or an
**ascent**: the 9:16 frame is a water column. The film is built from two lines and a circle.

| Motif | Meaning | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **The red line**: the air hose | Life, the father–son bond, the thread back up to the light | Opens the film as a Bass line; trails the diver everywhere; is CUT in s12; drifts down in s16; ends the film as a line again |
| **The pale line**: the lifting line | Greed, the client's reach into the deep | The winch line comes down in s10, is tied in s11, hauls the bell in s14 |
| **The circle** | Helmet port, moon, safe dial, lamp spot, the bell's mouth, the hole in the hull | Every important transition is a circle becoming another circle |

**Structure (the heartbeat).**

1. HOOK: s01, s02. The red line becomes a man going down. He takes cash from a man who never asks his name.
2. DESCENT: s03 to s05. The title, the long dive, the crime told as a Bass paper story, and the wreck looming.
3. DISCOVERY: s06 to s10. The holes were cut from the inside, the price was 31 men, the safe opens on his birthday, and the logbook holds the orders in the owner's own hand.
4. TWIST: s11 to s14. Two tugs. The client cuts the hose. One breath. The client hauls up the BELL, not the safe.
5. ECHO: s15, s16. "So I told him." The bell tolls, and the sea takes the client's hat down to the wreck.

**Continuity of time.** s01 is a flash-forward: the diver already sinking. Rising with his
bubbles to the surface takes us back to the evening of the dive (s02). From s03 on, time
runs straight.

film. Keep it ~10 px thick at zoom 1.

## 3. World

All sets come from `films/salvage/sets*.py` (see their module docstrings for the API: sea
surface and waterline, the boat, the descent column, the seabed, the Meridian wreck outside
and inside, the cabin, and the props). Underwater, world y is depth. Use the sets'
`backdrop`/depth helpers so the water color matches across scenes:

- aqua near the surface;
- teal in mid-water;
- ink black at the wreck, lit only by the helmet lamp, a warm amber cone
  (`atmos.beam` from `costume.helmet_lamp(sk)`).

Light plan:

| Scenes | Light |
|---|---|
| s02 | dusk with a low moon |
| s03 | surface god rays fade out by mid-descent |
| s05–s13 | lamp only, plus faint far rays at the top of the frame |
| s14, s15 | night with a high moon |
| s16 | moonlit rays reaching down |

Camera: slow eased moves, vertical journeys. Tense moments get `Camera.shaken`, ≤ 4 px.

## 4. Storyboard

Times are scene-local seconds. `[word@t]` marks the narration word to sync on
(`T.at('word')`). **IN/OUT** are the handoff contracts between neighbouring scenes: screen
positions in 1080×1920 frame px. Transitions are composited by the renderer from both
scenes' handles, so **keep animating through the handles**: the scene must render
naturally for `t < 0` and `t > T.duration` within `T.handle_in` and `T.handle_out`.

## 5. Sound plan (for the sound team)

- **Score.** Low drones in D minor. Sub pressure that grows with depth, and a slow pulse (heartbeat or a ship's engine).
  - Bass interludes (s04, s07, s09 spiral) get a dry, plucked/col legno ostinato, like a jazz-noir title (Bass/Bernstein feel).
  - The bell's pitch is the film's tonic. The bell is heard muffled in s05, tolls fully in s14 and s15, and its echo carries s16.
- **Underwater.** Muffled, low-passed world.
  - The diver's breathing: regulator-less free-flow hiss in, exhale bubbles.
  - The breath STOPS at the cut (s12 6.0) and is replaced by silence and heartbeat (s13).
  - Bubbles return as a single big release when he sheds the weights.
- **Surface.** Swell slaps on the hull, the creak of rope and davit, the pump flywheel's chuff, wind, distant gulls at dusk (s02 only).
- **Key sync hits.**

  | Scene | Hits |
  |---|---|
  | s02 | helmet clank |
  | s03 | lamp click |
  | s09 | tumbler clicks |
  | s11 | two tugs |
  | s12 | knife draw and cut; air hiss |
  | s13 | weights drop |
  | s14, s15 | bell tolls |

- **Narration.** A dry close voice over everything. Duck music −6 dB under the voice. Mix target −14 LUFS integrated.
